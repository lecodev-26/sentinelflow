# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful and constructive open-source community.

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not people.
- Welcome different experience levels.
- Do not harass, discriminate against or threaten other participants.

Unacceptable behavior may result in moderation or removal from project spaces.

If you experience or witness abusive behavior, contact the maintainer privately through the repository's available contact/reporting channels.
